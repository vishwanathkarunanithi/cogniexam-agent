 COGNIEXAM: SMART QUESTION PAPER BLOOM'S AUDIT & ANSWER KEY GENERATOR
 Instructions for Evaluation & Demo
================================================================================

Dear Mam,

This project directly solves your problem statement:
"When a faculty or evaluator uploads any examination question paper,
 the system automatically audits and computes its Bloom's Taxonomy score
 and synthesizes a complete, canonical Model Answer Key with step-by-step
 marking rubrics."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO RUN THE APPLICATION (1 STEP):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On Windows:
  Simply double-click the file named:
  -->  RUN_APP.bat

It will automatically launch your web browser at: http://localhost:8501

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO TEST THE CORE PROBLEM STATEMENT (IN 1 MINUTE):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In Tab 1 ("Upload Question Paper ➔ Bloom Score & Answer Key"):
   - Click the button:
     "📋 Load Sample University Question Paper"
     (Or upload your own Question Paper PDF / text).

2. Click the big blue button:
   "🚀 Analyze Question Paper & Generate Answer Key".

3. Within seconds, the AI Agent Swarm delivers:
   a. Overall Bloom's Cognitive Score (e.g. 4.2 / 6.0) & OBE Balance Verdict.
   b. Visual Bar Chart & Tier breakdown:
      - Lower-Order Thinking (L1 Remember & L2 Understand) %
      - Middle-Order Thinking (L3 Apply & L4 Analyze) %
      - Higher-Order Thinking (L5 Evaluate & L6 Create) %
   c. Individual Question Cards:
      - Bloom's Level Badge (L1 to L6) with Action Verbs.
      - Pedagogical Justification explaining why it belongs to that level.
   d. Complete Answer Key:
      - Canonical Model Answers for every question.
      - Granular Step-by-Step Marking Rubrics (e.g., Step 1: 3 marks, Step 2: 4 marks).
   e. Download Buttons:
      - Instant download of the full certified report in Markdown or JSON!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDITIONAL CAPABILITIES IN OTHER TABS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tab 2: Author a brand new exam paper from syllabus with Bloom constraints.
- Tab 3: Automated student answer grading with partial credit and diagnostic feedback.
- Tab 4: Cloud-native deployment architecture on Google Cloud.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIVE CLOUD INTEGRATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is connected directly to Google Gemini Cloud AI via the configured
API key, and also includes an intelligent local fallback so it functions seamlessly
anywhere, anytime.

Thank you!
